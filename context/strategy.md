This workspace is focused on **sales + growth** for hrmny (including PR as a hrmny service line). No Cream, no music.

## Strategic Priorities

1. **Build a predictable qualified-lead engine (top of funnel)**
   - Run three acquisition lanes in parallel:
     - **Industry scanning → target list → Apollo/LinkedIn → outreach → qualify → tailor offer**
     - **Apollo-first list building (intent + fit signals) → outreach → qualify → tailor offer**
     - **Relationship-led intros + selective outbound**
   - Keep the “no-go” filter active:
     - **No low-budget startups**
     - **No small local brands (unless clearly global-level budgets)**
     - **No alcohol**
     - **No tobacco**

2. **Maintain a clean, decision-ready pipeline (Asana as source of truth)**
   - Pipeline hygiene is non-negotiable: stages, next steps, owners, and expected close dates always current.
   - Focus stages for weekly steering: **Qualified and beyond**.
   - Keep a clear separation between:
     - **Retainer**
     - **Project / campaign**
     - **PR (standalone retainer OR included in a wider scope depending on client)**

3. **Hit consistent meeting volume (leading indicator for growth)**
   - Target **2–3 qualified meetings per week** with the right senior stakeholders (C-suite / decision owners).
   - Improve velocity:
     - Reduce stalled deals
     - Tighten follow-up discipline
     - Keep momentum after “Qualified” and “Submitted”

4. **Increase conversion without discounting the business**
   - Address the current constraint chain:
     - **Not enough qualified leads → stalled deals / slow follow-up → pricing resistance**
   - Tighten qualification, run stronger discovery, and present clearer value-to-price logic before any discount discussion.
   - Stay within governance:
     - Discounts above agreed thresholds require escalation (CFO + joint partner sign-off where applicable).

5. **Standardise the commercial engine: from qualified lead → signed proposal**
   - Make the system repeatable end-to-end:
     - Qualification → scope design → pricing logic → proposal build → revisions → close → handover
   - Ensure proposals can be built quickly and consistently in your existing style and structure:
     - Clear scope language
     - Clear pricing logic
     - Clear commercial terms
   - Long-term target: support the full flow from lead gen → proposal generation (including your pricing + scoping method).

6. **Fix the sales-to-delivery gap (handover standard)**
   - Today’s biggest weakness to solve in 2026:
     - **scoping vs delivery vs reporting for analysis**
   - Create a consistent “Won Deal Handover Pack” so delivery matches what was sold.
   - Handover must clearly route responsibilities to:
     - **CS lead: Youssef**
     - **Ops: Molham**
     - **PRD/Growth: Dana** (only when PR is in scope)

7. **Focus sectors where the UAE is investing and brands are competing**
   - Recommended 2026 outbound focus lanes (UAE-first):
     - **Retail + consumer experience** (incl. premium retail + shopping destinations + F&B retail)
     - **Sports / wellness / movements** (brands building communities + performance-meets-lifestyle)
     - **Automotive (incl. EV entrants)** as a secondary lane
   - Rationale:
     - Dubai retail is still expanding aggressively and experience-led (retail + F&B + leisure), with strong demand for prime space and high visitor volumes.  
     - MENA growth continues to be driven by the shift to digital and rising advertising revenue.  

## What Success Looks Like

- **Meeting volume:** 2–3 qualified senior meetings/week (tracked weekly).
- **Pipeline clarity:** Asana is always current; stages + next steps are real and owned.
- **Pipeline coverage:** enough qualified pipeline to avoid “panic pitching” (≥3× monthly topline goal as a rule of thumb).
- **Speed:** clear next step on every active opportunity; no deal sits without movement.
- **Conversion:** win-rate improves through better discovery + better offers (not through discounting).
- **Retainers:** more retainers (quantity) **and** bigger retainers (value / upsell).
- **Commercial quality:** proposals are consistent, sharp, and fast to produce — with clean scope, pricing logic, and terms.
- **Sales-to-delivery alignment:** every won deal has a handover package so Ops/CS can deliver what was sold.
