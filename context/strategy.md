## Qualification Bar (Decided)

**Minimum threshold: BUAF score of 7/10, or at minimum 3 of 4 criteria met.**

Each criterion is scored 0-3:

| Criterion | 0 (No) | 1 (Weak) | 2 (Moderate) | 3 (Strong) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Budget (B)** | Cannot afford hrmny | Budget unclear, small company | Mid-size, likely has budget but unconfirmed | Known budget, large brand, clear marketing spend |
| **Urgency (U)** | No trigger visible | Possible future need (6+ months out) | Near-term trigger (1-3 months) | Immediate need: launch, RFP, agency review, event |
| **Access (A)** | No contact path | Have company only, no contact | Have a contact but not decision-maker | Decision-maker identified with email or LinkedIn |
| **Fit (F)** | Needs services hrmny does not offer | Partial overlap | Good overlap, 1-2 service lines | Perfect fit across multiple service lines |

**Decision rules:**
- **Score 10-12 (Hot):** Pursue immediately. Tailor offer, book meeting.
- **Score 7-9 (Warm):** Worth pursuing. Address weakest criterion. Invest time in outreach.
- **Score 4-6 (Cool):** Park. Monitor for changes. Do not invest heavy outbound effort.
- **Score 0-3 (Cold):** Do not pursue unless circumstances change materially.

**Hard rule:** Never invest in a tailored offer (custom proposal, scope design, pricing) unless the lead scores 7+ or has all four criteria at minimum "Weak" (1) with at least one at "Strong" (3).

## Follow-Up Cadence (Decided)

**Standard multi-channel outreach sequence:**

| Day | Action | Channel | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Day 0** | Initial email | Email | Personalised cold email per outreach guidelines |
| **Day 1** | LinkedIn connection request | LinkedIn | 300-char personalised request. Do not pitch. |
| **Day 3** | LinkedIn follow-up message | LinkedIn | Only if connection accepted. CTA for call. |
| **Day 7** | Second email | Email | New angle or additional value. Not a "just checking in." |
| **Day 14** | Break-up email | Email | Final outreach. Clear, respectful close. Leave door open. |
| **Day 21** | Decision: re-engage or park | Internal | Review signals. If any engagement (open, click, profile view), re-engage with new angle. If zero engagement, park the lead. |

**Cadence rules:**
- **Reply at any point:** Stop the sequence. Respond personally. Move to conversation.
- **LinkedIn connection accepted but no reply to follow-up:** Wait 5 days, send one more LinkedIn message with a fresh angle. If still no reply, park.
- **Email bounce:** Immediately pivot to LinkedIn-only. Flag for alternative contact search.
- **Out-of-office reply:** Pause sequence. Resume 2 days after their return date.
- **"Not interested" reply:** Acknowledge gracefully. Remove from active sequence. Log reason if given.
- **"Not now" reply:** Acknowledge. Set a reminder for the timeframe they suggest. Move to parked with a re-engage date.

**Deal health definition:** A deal is "healthy" if it has had a meaningful interaction (not just an open) within the last 7 days. Any deal with no interaction for 14+ days is flagged as stalled and requires action or a decision to park.

## Won Deal Handover Template (Decided)

Every won deal must produce a **Handover Pack** before delivery begins. The pack is created by the salesperson (Ayham) and handed to the delivery team.

### Core fields (mandatory)

| Section | Contents |
|---|---|
| **1. Client Overview** | Company name, industry, key contacts (name, title, email, phone), decision-maker vs. day-to-day contact |
| **2. Scope of Work** | Exact services sold (SMM, PR, campaigns, branding, activations, production). Monthly deliverables with quantities. What is explicitly out of scope. |
| **3. Commercial Terms** | Contract value (total + monthly if retainer). Payment terms and schedule. Discount applied (if any) with approval reference. Contract duration and renewal terms. |
| **4. Brand Context** | Brand positioning and tone notes. Key competitors. Current marketing setup (in-house team, other agencies). Why they chose hrmny (buying triggers). |
| **5. Expectations & KPIs** | What the client expects to see (stated in their words). Success metrics agreed during sales. Reporting cadence and format agreed. |
| **6. Timeline** | Start date. Key milestones or deadlines (launches, campaigns, events). Onboarding meeting date. |
| **7. Risk Flags** | Any concerns raised during sales. Pricing sensitivity notes. Relationship dynamics (who is the champion, who is the skeptic). Scope creep risks. |

### Routing

| Role | Receives | Responsible for |
|---|---|---|
| **Youssef (CS lead)** | Full pack | Client relationship, reporting, day-to-day delivery oversight |
| **Molham (Ops)** | Scope + commercial + timeline | Resource allocation, production scheduling, margin tracking |
| **Dana (PRD/Growth)** | Full pack (PR scope only) | PR strategy and execution (only when PR is in scope) |

### Handover process

1. Ayham completes the Handover Pack within 48 hours of deal signing.
2. Handover meeting (30 min) with Youssef + Molham (+ Dana if PR is in scope).
3. CS lead (Youssef) confirms receipt and flags any gaps within 24 hours.
4. Client onboarding meeting scheduled within 5 business days of signing.

## Deal Velocity Rules

Maximum allowable time per pipeline stage before escalation. If a deal exceeds the max, it must either be actively progressed or moved to a "Parked" status with a reason.

| Stage | Max Days | Escalation Action |
|---|---|---|
| **Researched** (Google Sheets) | 5 days | Must be approved or rejected. If untouched after 5 days, auto-flag in `/dashboard`. |
| **Contacts Found** (Google Sheets) | 3 days | Contact must be approved or rejected. Blocking outreach. |
| **Outreach Sent** (Google Sheets) | 7 days | If no reply after 7 days, auto-mark "No Response". Follow-up cadence should have already triggered. |
| **Connected** (Asana) | 10 days | Must have a discovery call booked or be moved to Qualified/Parked. Flag in `/pipeline-review`. |
| **Qualified** (Asana) | 14 days | Proposal must be in progress or submitted. If stalled, escalate: is this deal real? |
| **Submitted** (Asana) | 14 days | Follow up for feedback. If no response after 14 days, send break-up message and consider parking. |
| **Negotiation** (Asana) | 7 days | Must be moving toward close. Stalled negotiation = competitor risk or budget issue. Escalate. |

**Velocity health dashboard (tracked weekly):**
- Average days in stage (per stage)
- Number of deals exceeding max days
- Deals with no activity in 14+ days (across all stages)
