### Funnel benchmarks (targets)

| Transition | Target Rate | Industry Benchmark |
|---|---|---|
| Researched → Approved | 60-70% | Depends on research quality |
| Approved → Contacts Found | 80%+ | Apollo enrichment success rate |
| Contact Approved → Outreach Sent | 95%+ | Should be near-automatic |
| Outreach Sent → Replied | 15%+ | 10-20% is typical for cold outreach |
| Replied → Connected | 50%+ | Depends on reply quality |
| Connected → Qualified | 40%+ | Discovery call conversion |
| Qualified → Submitted | 80%+ | Should be high if qualification is strong |
| Submitted → Won | 40%+ | Current: ~26.7%. Target: 40%+ |

### How to read this

- **Top of funnel (Researched → Contact Approved):** Volume and quality of lead generation. If Approved rate is low, research targeting needs refinement.
- **Middle of funnel (Outreach Sent → Connected):** Outreach effectiveness. If reply rate is below 10%, messaging or targeting needs work.
- **Bottom of funnel (Qualified → Won):** Sales execution. If win rate is below 30%, discovery, proposals, or pricing logic needs improvement.
