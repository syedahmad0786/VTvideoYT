## Organization Overview

**hrmny** is a Dubai-based **creative agency born from culture and built for impact**, working with brands across the UAE and wider GCC. We operate at the intersection of **strategy, creative execution, and earned attention**—building work that moves with relevance and lands with precision.

We have presence in **Dubai and Riyadh**, and we’re built for brands that want to operate at the speed of culture while still demanding operational reliability.

**How we’re different (in plain terms):**
- **Culture-fluent thinking** (we don’t outsource relevance)
- **Fast but not rushed** delivery (tight operations, high standards)
- **Multi-sensory storytelling** (creative that feels designed, not generic)

## Products / Services / Focus Areas

Our primary revenue services:

- **Social Media Retainers (SMM):** Strategy + content systems + community (monthly retainers)
- **Campaigns & Production:** Big ideas translated into full-funnel campaigns and content production
- **Public Relations (core service):** Press office, creator partnerships, events/experiences, community building, earned visibility
- **Branding / Identity:** Brand identity, positioning translation, visual systems
- **Activations / Experiences:** Cultural moments, launches, experiential formats (often paired with PR and content)

Supporting/adjacent capabilities (when needed for the work):
- Strategy and comms planning (roadmaps, content ecosystems)
- Content production formats (film, mobile-first, photography)
- Partnerships and creator ecosystems

## Key Context

- Team size: ~33 full-time employees
- Current scale: ~AED 10M/year baseline
- We’re in a growth phase and need to **expand pipeline and conversion** to scale.

**Who we serve (ICP)**
- Global brands based in the UAE
- Local brands with meaningful marketing budgets
- Category sweet spots: **sports & movements, lifestyle, music culture, automotive, retail, F&B**

**Typical commercial ranges (AED)**
- SMM retainers: ~AED 25K/month (average)
