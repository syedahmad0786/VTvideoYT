# Ideal Customer Profile & Qualification Criteria

> This file defines who hrmny targets, who to avoid, and what qualifies a lead as worth pursuing. Commands like `/find-leads`, `/daily-research`, `/process-intent-leads`, and `/promote-lead` read this to ensure consistent targeting and qualification.

---

## Fast Reject — Immediate Disqualifiers

**Stop before scoring.** If ANY of these are true, reject immediately without running BUAF:

| # | Signal | Why |
|---|--------|-----|
| 1 | Alcohol or tobacco brand | Firm policy — no exceptions |
| 2 | Fewer than 20 employees AND no visible marketing spend | Cannot afford hrmny's rates |
| 3 | Startup pre-Series A with no established brand presence | Budget and timeline mismatch |
| 4 | Company headquartered outside GCC with zero UAE/GCC operations or plans | No regional relevance |
| 5 | Government entity or semi-government (unless direct referral with named budget holder) | Procurement cycles too long, payment risk |
| 6 | Brand explicitly focused on performance marketing only (no brand/creative need) | Service misalignment — hrmny is not a performance agency |
| 7 | Already working with a top-tier agency on a multi-year retainer with no signs of dissatisfaction | Low probability of switch |
| 8 | Company in active legal/financial distress (layoffs, restructuring, lawsuits in news) | Payment risk, decision paralysis |
| 9 | Contact is a freelancer, intern, or junior coordinator with no path to budget holder | No access |
| 10 | RFP/pitch requires spec creative work before commercial terms are discussed | Unpaid pitching — not how hrmny operates |

**Rule**: If a fast-reject signal is present, log the reason and move on. Do not spend time researching further.

## Speed Qualification Checklist — 30 Seconds

Before running full BUAF, answer these five yes/no questions. **If 4+ are "Yes", proceed to full BUAF. If 3, proceed with caution. If 2 or fewer, park or reject.**

| # | Question | Y/N |
|---|----------|-----|
| 1 | Is this a brand we would be proud to have in our portfolio? | |
| 2 | Could they realistically spend AED 150K+ with us in the first 12 months? | |
| 3 | Is there a trigger event visible (launch, rebrand, market entry, agency gap, campaign push)? | |
| 4 | Can we identify a decision-maker or senior influencer by name? | |
| 5 | Do they need at least one of hrmny's core services (SMM, PR, campaigns, branding, activations)? | |

**4-5 Yes** = Green light, run full BUAF
**3 Yes** = Proceed with caution, flag the gap
**0-2 Yes** = Park or reject — not worth the time right now

## Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)

### Who We Target

- **Global brands operating in the UAE** — multinational companies with regional marketing teams or agencies in Dubai/GCC
- **Strong local/regional brands with meaningful marketing budgets** — not startups, not micro-businesses
- **Brands that need to move at the speed of culture** — they care about relevance, not just reach

### Minimum Company Size Indicators

| Indicator | Minimum threshold | Strong signal |
|-----------|------------------|---------------|
| Employee count | 50+ (global) or 15+ (regional/ME office) | 200+ global or 30+ regional |
| Estimated revenue | AED 20M+/year (or USD 5M+) | AED 100M+ or publicly traded |
| Marketing team size | At least 1 dedicated marketing hire in region | 3+ marketing/comms staff in UAE |
| Social media presence | Active accounts with regular posting | 10K+ followers, running paid campaigns |
| Agency history | Has worked with agencies before | Currently with an agency, open to change |
| Hiring signals | Any marketing/comms hiring in UAE | Multiple open roles in brand/comms |

**Note**: These are indicators, not hard cutoffs. A 30-person company with a AED 500K campaign budget clears the bar. A 500-person company with no marketing function does not.

### Company Signals That Indicate Fit

- Active or growing presence in UAE/GCC
- Investing in brand building (not purely performance marketing)
- Running campaigns, activations, or content programs
- Hiring for marketing/comms roles in the region
- Launching new products, entering new markets, or repositioning
- Working with agencies they may be outgrowing or misaligned with
- Recently closed funding (Series B+) with brand-building on the roadmap
- Visible presence at UAE trade shows, expos, or cultural events

### Stakeholder Profile — By Company Size

| Company type | Employee count | Target title (minimum) | Examples |
|-------------|---------------|----------------------|----------|
| **Enterprise** (global HQ or large regional) | 500+ | VP or above | VP Marketing MENA, CMO, SVP Brand |
| **Mid-market** (established regional presence) | 100-500 | Director or above | Marketing Director, Brand Director, Head of Comms |
| **Regional / Boutique** (strong brand, lean team) | 15-100 | Head of / Manager (senior) | Head of Marketing, Senior Brand Manager, MD (if marketing-involved) |

**Decision level**: Must have budget authority or direct access to budget holder. If the contact cannot approve a AED 25K/month spend without escalating 3 levels, they are too junior.

### Primary Lanes

1. **Retail + Consumer Experience** — premium retail, shopping destinations, F&B retail, lifestyle retail. Dubai retail is expanding aggressively and experience-led.
2. **Sports / Wellness / Movements** — brands building communities, performance-meets-lifestyle, event-driven brands. Strong overlap with hrmny's creative DNA.

### Secondary Lane

### Opportunistic (pursue if inbound or warm intro only)

- Tech / consumer tech with lifestyle positioning
- Hospitality / luxury with experiential marketing needs
- Real estate developers with premium lifestyle branding needs

## No-Go List

Do not pursue. Filter these out immediately — no exceptions unless explicitly overridden by a Managing Partner.

| Category | Criteria | Why |
|----------|----------|-----|
| **Alcohol brands** | Any brand where alcohol is the primary product or revenue driver | Firm policy |
| **Tobacco / vape brands** | Any brand producing or primarily distributing tobacco or vaping products | Firm policy |
| **Low-budget startups** | Pre-Series A, fewer than 15 employees, no visible marketing budget, bootstrapped with no agency history | Cannot afford hrmny's rates, will drain resources with scope creep |
| **Small local brands** | UAE-only brand with fewer than 50 employees AND annual revenue below AED 10M AND no global parent/backer | Budget insufficient — unless they demonstrably have global-level budgets (e.g., backed by a holding group) |
| **Crypto / Web3 projects** | Token-based projects, NFT platforms, or DAOs without an established consumer brand | High churn, payment risk, reputation risk |
| **Political campaigns or advocacy** | Any political party, candidate, or politically charged advocacy group | Reputation risk |
| **Spec-work pitches** | Any RFP requiring full creative delivery before commercial agreement | Unpaid labour, devalues the agency |

**Grey zone**: If a lead is borderline (e.g., a local brand with a well-funded parent company), note it and flag for Ayham's judgment. Do not auto-reject without explanation.

## BUAF Qualification Framework — Numeric Scoring

A lead must be scored on **all four dimensions**. Each dimension scores **1-10**. Total score drives the action.

### 1. Budget (B) — Score 1-10

_Can they realistically afford hrmny's services?_

| Score | Definition |
|-------|-----------|
| **9-10** | Confirmed budget allocated for agency services. Known marketing spend at hrmny's level or above. Public financials or category norms confirm AED 250K+ annual agency spend. |
| **7-8** | Strong budget signals: large company, active campaigns, working with agencies, hiring for marketing. No confirmed number but very likely at threshold. |
| **5-6** | Moderate signals: established brand, some marketing activity, size suggests budget exists but unconfirmed. Might need discovery to confirm. |
| **3-4** | Weak signals: small team, limited visible marketing, unclear if they invest in agencies. Could afford a single project but unlikely a retainer. |
| **1-2** | No budget signals: startup, very small, no marketing presence, likely below AED 100K total annual marketing spend. |

**Reference thresholds (minimum to engage)**:
- SMM retainer: ~AED 25K/month (AED 300K/year)
- PR retainer: ~AED 15-20K/month (AED 180-240K/year)
- Campaign / project: ~AED 250K+
- Branding: ~AED 40-80K
- Activation: ~AED 150K+ (varies significantly)
- Bundled retainer (SMM + PR): ~AED 40K+/month (AED 480K+/year)

### 2. Urgency (U) — Score 1-10

_Is there a near-term need or trigger? What is the timeline?_

| Score | Timeline | Definition |
|-------|----------|-----------|
| **9-10** | **0-30 days** | Active RFP out, agency contract ending, launch date set, event imminent, or they reached out to us. Decision will be made this month. |
| **7-8** | **30-60 days** | Confirmed upcoming need: product launch next quarter, campaign planning underway, known agency review in progress, seasonal push approaching (Ramadan, DSF). |
| **5-6** | **60-90 days** | Trigger visible but timeline soft: new market entry planned, brand refresh discussed publicly, hiring suggests scaling, competitor moves creating pressure. |
| **3-4** | **90-180 days** | General growth signals but no specific trigger: expanding in region, broad statements about marketing investment. Worth monitoring. |
| **1-2** | **180+ days or none** | No urgency signal. Established brand, stable agency relationship, no visible change catalyst. Park for later. |

**Key triggers to watch for** (each adds urgency):
- Upcoming launch, campaign, event, or rebrand
- Agency contract expiring or relationship souring
- New market entry (UAE/GCC)
- Seasonal push (Ramadan prep starts 60-90 days before)
- Leadership change in marketing/brand team
- Competitor just launched a major campaign
- IPO or funding round (need to elevate brand)

### 3. Access (A) — Score 1-10

_Can we reach a decision-maker or someone with direct influence?_

| Score | Definition |
|-------|-----------|
| **9-10** | Direct warm intro to decision-maker, or decision-maker reached out to us. Mutual connection willing to introduce. Previous relationship exists. |
| **7-8** | Decision-maker identified by name with LinkedIn profile and/or verified email. No warm path but clear cold outreach route to the right person. |
| **5-6** | Senior contact identified but may not be the decision-maker. OR decision-maker identified but no contact details found — LinkedIn connection request is the only path. |
| **3-4** | Company identified but no specific contact found yet. General company emails only. Will require research to find the right person. |
| **1-2** | Cannot identify any relevant contact. Company has no visible marketing team in UAE. No LinkedIn presence. Gatekeepers likely. |

**Access by company type (minimum contact level)**:
| Company type | Minimum viable contact | Ideal contact |
|-------------|----------------------|---------------|
| Enterprise (500+) | VP Marketing / Brand Director | CMO / SVP Brand / Regional MD |
| Mid-market (100-500) | Marketing Director / Head of Comms | CMO / Marketing VP / GM |
| Regional / Boutique (15-100) | Head of Marketing / Senior Brand Manager | MD / Founder (if marketing-involved) |

**Access quality multiplier**: A warm intro is worth roughly 3x a cold outreach in terms of conversion probability. Weight this in prioritisation.

### 4. Fit (F) — Score 1-10

_Does the brand need services hrmny actually delivers? Would we be proud of this work?_

| Score | Definition |
|-------|-----------|
| **9-10** | Perfect alignment: brand clearly needs 2+ hrmny services, creative values match, would be a portfolio highlight, sector is a 2026 priority lane. |
| **7-8** | Strong alignment: needs at least one core service, brand positioning is compatible, sector fits or is adjacent to priority lanes. |
| **5-6** | Moderate alignment: needs one service but scope may be narrow. Brand is acceptable but not a portfolio standout. Sector is outside priority lanes but not a no-go. |
| **3-4** | Weak alignment: need is peripheral to hrmny's strengths (e.g., only need media buying, only need a one-off logo). Limited creative excitement. |
| **1-2** | Misaligned: brand needs services hrmny does not offer, or the brand's positioning conflicts with hrmny's creative values. |

**Core services for fit assessment**:
- Social Media Management (SMM) — strategy + content systems + community
- Public Relations — press office, creator partnerships, events, community, earned visibility
- Campaigns & Production — full-funnel campaigns, content production
- Branding / Identity — brand identity, positioning, visual systems
- Activations / Experiences — cultural moments, launches, experiential formats

## BUAF Scoring Summary & Action Matrix

### How to Score

Add all four dimension scores for a **total out of 40**.

| Total Score | Label | Action |
|-------------|-------|--------|
| **33-40** | **Hot** | Prioritise immediately. Draft tailored outreach, fast-track to meeting. Allocate time for discovery and proposal. |
| **25-32** | **Warm** | Worth pursuing actively. Address any dimension scoring below 6. Put in next outreach batch. |
| **17-24** | **Cool** | Park and monitor. Add to watchlist. Revisit if a trigger event surfaces or access improves. Do not invest heavy time. |
| **1-16** | **Cold** | Do not pursue. Log the reason. Only revisit if circumstances fundamentally change. |

### Minimum Dimension Thresholds

Even if the total is high, a lead with **any single dimension scoring 2 or below** should be flagged for review. A "Hot" total with Access = 1 means you have no way in — it is not actually actionable.

| Dimension | Minimum to proceed | Below minimum action |
|-----------|-------------------|---------------------|
| Budget | 4 | Park unless other signals are exceptionally strong |
| Urgency | 3 | Add to monitoring list, do not pursue actively |
| Access | 4 | Research contacts before scoring further; if still below 4, park |
| Fit | 5 | Likely not a good match — park unless Budget + Urgency are both 8+ |

## Deal-Size Estimation Matrix

Use this to estimate expected deal value when qualifying leads. Cross-reference the **sector** with the **likely service need** to get an indicative AED range.

### By Service Type

| Service | Retainer (annual) | Project (one-off) | Notes |
|---------|-------------------|-------------------|-------|
| **SMM** | AED 250K-400K | AED 50K-120K (audit/strategy only) | Retainer is the target — projects are entry points |
| **PR** | AED 180K-300K | AED 80K-200K (launch campaign) | Standalone or bundled with SMM |
| **Campaigns & Production** | N/A (project-based) | AED 250K-800K | One-off or seasonal. Can recur annually |
| **Branding / Identity** | N/A (project-based) | AED 40K-150K | One-off. Often leads to retainer work |
| **Activations** | N/A (project-based) | AED 150K-500K+ | Highly variable. Often paired with PR |
| **Bundled (SMM + PR)** | AED 450K-700K | N/A | Premium clients. Highest LTV |
| **Full scope (SMM + PR + Content)** | AED 600K-1M+ | N/A | Enterprise clients. Rare but highest value |

### By Sector (typical first-year value)

| Sector | Small engagement | Typical engagement | Large engagement |
|--------|-----------------|-------------------|-----------------|
| **Retail + Consumer Experience** | AED 80K (seasonal campaign) | AED 300K-500K (SMM + campaign) | AED 700K+ (full retainer + activations) |
| **Sports / Wellness / Movements** | AED 60K (event activation) | AED 200K-400K (PR + content) | AED 500K+ (full retainer + events) |
| **Automotive / EV** | AED 150K (launch campaign) | AED 400K-600K (campaign + PR) | AED 800K+ (retainer + launches + activations) |
| **Tech / Consumer Tech** | AED 50K (social audit) | AED 250K-350K (SMM retainer) | AED 500K+ (multi-service) |
| **Hospitality / Luxury** | AED 100K (opening campaign) | AED 300K-500K (PR + social) | AED 600K+ (ongoing retainer) |

**How to use this**: When scoring Budget, cross-reference the estimated deal size with what the company can likely afford. If the typical engagement for their sector is AED 400K and they show budget signals supporting that level, Budget scores 7-8.

When assessing what a lead might need, map to hrmny's service lines:

## Worked Example

**Company**: Lucid Motors — new EV entrant, just opened a Dubai showroom.

**Fast Reject**: None triggered. Proceed.

**Speed Qualification**:
1. Proud to have in portfolio? **Yes** (premium EV brand)
2. Could spend AED 150K+ in 12 months? **Yes** (publicly traded, luxury segment)
3. Trigger event? **Yes** (showroom opening, market entry)
4. Decision-maker identifiable? **Yes** (Head of Marketing ME on LinkedIn)
5. Need hrmny's services? **Yes** (launch campaign, PR, social)

**Result**: 5/5 Yes. Green light for full BUAF.

**BUAF Score**:
- Budget: **8** — public company, luxury segment, active marketing in other markets. Highly likely at threshold.
- Urgency: **9** — just opened showroom, active market entry, need agency support now.
- Access: **7** — Head of Marketing ME identified on LinkedIn. No warm intro but clear path.
- Fit: **9** — automotive is a 2026 lane, need campaign + PR + social, brand aligns with hrmny's creative values.

**Total**: 33/40 = **Hot**

**Estimated deal size**: Automotive sector, likely Campaign + PR = AED 400-600K first engagement.

**Action**: Prioritise. Draft tailored outreach referencing showroom opening and market entry needs.
