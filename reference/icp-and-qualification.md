# Ideal Customer Profile & Qualification Criteria

> This file defines who hrmny targets, who to avoid, and what qualifies a lead as worth pursuing. Commands like `/find-leads` and `/promote-lead` read this to ensure consistent targeting.

---

## Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)

### Who we target

- **Global brands operating in the UAE** — multinational companies with regional marketing teams or agencies in Dubai/GCC
- **Strong local/regional brands with meaningful marketing budgets** — not startups, not micro-businesses
- **Brands that need to move at the speed of culture** — they care about relevance, not just reach

### Company signals that indicate fit

- Active or growing presence in UAE/GCC
- Investing in brand building (not purely performance marketing)
- Running campaigns, activations, or content programs
- Hiring for marketing/comms roles in the region
- Launching new products, entering new markets, or repositioning
- Working with agencies they may be outgrowing or misaligned with

### Stakeholder profile

- **Primary targets**: CMO, Head of Marketing, Marketing Director, Brand Director, Communications Director
- **Secondary targets**: Regional Marketing Manager, Head of PR/Comms, Brand Manager (senior)
- **Decision level**: Must have budget authority or direct access to budget holder
- Typically C-suite or one level below in regional/ME operations

## Qualification Criteria

A lead must meet **all four** of these before investing time in a tailored offer:

### 1. Budget (B)
- Can they realistically afford hrmny's services?
- Minimum thresholds (approximate):
  - SMM retainer: ~AED 25K/month
  - PR retainer: ~AED 15-20K/month
  - Campaign/project: ~AED 250K+
  - Branding: ~AED 40-80K
- If budget is unclear, the company's size, sector, and known marketing activity should suggest they operate at this level

### 2. Urgency (U)
- Is there a near-term need or trigger?
  - Upcoming launch, campaign, event, or rebrand
  - Agency change or dissatisfaction
  - New market entry (UAE/GCC)
  - Seasonal push (Ramadan, DSF, summer)
- No urgency = low priority (park, don't pursue aggressively)

### 3. Access (A)
- Can we reach a decision-maker or someone with direct influence?
- Do we have a contact name, email, or LinkedIn profile?
- Is there a warm path (mutual connection, previous interaction)?
- Cold outreach is fine if the contact is senior enough

### 4. Fit (F)
- Does the brand need services hrmny actually delivers?
  - SMM, PR, campaigns, branding, activations, content production
- Is there creative alignment? (Would we be proud of this work?)
- Is the brand's positioning compatible with hrmny's style?

### Qualification scoring

| Rating | Criteria met | Action |
|---|---|---|
| **Hot** | All 4 (B+U+A+F) | Prioritise — pursue immediately, tailor offer |
| **Warm** | 3 of 4 | Worth pursuing — address the missing element |
| **Cool** | 2 of 4 | Park — monitor, don't invest heavy time yet |
| **Cold** | 1 or fewer | Do not pursue unless circumstances change |

## Service Alignment Quick Reference

When assessing what a lead might need, map to hrmny's service lines:

| Signal | Likely service |
|---|---|
| Active on social but inconsistent/generic | **SMM retainer** |
| Launching product, entering market, big moment | **Campaign + Production** |
| No press coverage, weak earned media | **PR retainer** |
| Visual identity feels dated or inconsistent | **Branding / Identity** |
| Running events, experiential, community building | **Activations / Experiences** |
| Multiple needs across the above | **Bundled retainer (SMM + PR + Content)** |
